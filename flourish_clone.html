Frequently asked questions

Scrollytelling is a storytelling technique where visualizations, text, and media change dynamically as you scroll. In Flourish, you can combine charts, maps, and narrative slides into a seamless, interactive experience without coding.



Scrollytelling is available for users on our Enterprise and Publisher plans. This feature will automatically be available in your account – simply click on the story editor and start creating. If you’d like to unlock the full power of the Flourish platform and get access to Flourish scrollytelling, get in touch with our sales team.



Publisher and Enterprise customers can use the story editor to build engaging, scroll-based stories — no code required.

Head to the Story editor and add your slides and charts as usual. Once your visuals and captions are in place, open the navigation settings and select Scrolly to switch to scroll mode. Check out our help doc for more.
